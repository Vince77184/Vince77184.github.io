Hot Disk TPS 1500 Thermal Analyser

ISO 22007-2Acceptance testCertified referenceRepeatability

Summary

The Hot Disk TPS 1500 measures how heat spreads in a material: in a single test of a few tens of seconds it gives the thermal conductivity, diffusivity and volumetric heat capacity. A very thin nickel double spiral, sandwiched between two identical samples, is at the same time the heater and the thermometer. The instrument serves the collaborative HeatTransferID project (MSME, CERTES and THEMACS Ingénierie) on porous, fibrous, bio-based and recycled insulation materials.

I commissioned it at the industrial partner's site and wrote its 50-page lab notebook: theory, instrument description, how to choose probe, power and time, the measurement procedure and the acceptance checks.

On the bench

Acceptance on a certified AISI 316L stainless-steel reference: 10 measurements at 21.66 °C gave λ = 13.77 W/(m·K) (standard deviation 0.78 %), a = 3.606 mm²/s and ρCp = 3.816 MJ/(m³·K). Each channel of the probe switch was then checked one by one (13.61 to 13.78 W/(m·K)). The routine check before every session: at least three measurements on the reference, conforming if the deviation stays below the announced 5 % accuracy (1–2 % expected in practice).

Key results

  • Acceptance on certified 316L reference: λ = 13.77 W/(m·K), standard deviation 0.78 % over 10 runs
  • All five channels of the probe switch verified (13.61–13.78 W/(m·K))
  • 50-page lab notebook with parameter-selection method and routine checks

Method & equations

The probe resistance gives its mean temperature (TCR \(\alpha\approx0.005\ \mathrm{K^{-1}}\)), and the temperature rise follows the transient plane source solution (\(P\): heating power, \(r\): probe radius):

$$R(t)=R_0\left[1+\alpha\,\Delta T(t)\right],\qquad \Delta T(\tau)=\frac{P}{\pi^{3/2}\,r\,\lambda}\,D(\tau),\qquad \tau=\frac{\sqrt{a\,t}}{r},\qquad \rho C_p=\frac{\lambda}{a}$$

Choosing the measurement time: the total-to-characteristic time ratio must stay between 0.33 and 1, and the probed depth must remain inside the sample (\(L\), \(l\), \(e\): sample dimensions):

$$0.33\,\frac{r^{2}}{a}\le t\le\frac{r^{2}}{a},\qquad \Delta p=2\sqrt{a\,t}\ \le\ \min\left(e,\ \tfrac{L}{2}-r,\ \tfrac{l}{2}-r\right)$$
