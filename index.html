In parallel, installation prerequisites for about fifteen instruments (400 V three-phase, 8 bar air, cooling water, 2 t floor load, fire safety) and supplier comparison in France, Switzerland and the UK (€17k–100k), reported to the steering committee.

Procedures · Quality

Standardised test procedures

Four procedures written from the standards and from hands-on trials on the benches: impedance tube (ISO 10534-2), airflow resistivity (ISO 9053-1), open porosity, and transient plane source conductivity (ISO 22007-2). Each covers the method, acceptance criteria, quality checks and troubleshooting, so results stay comparable whoever runs the test.

Start-up review · Industrial communication

Microwave reactor bench

Technical review of a SAIREM MicroChem bench (0–200 W solid-state, 2.45 GHz) before start-up: hardware inventory, documentation, control software and its Modbus RTU / RS485 link, plus a beginner's guide for the team.

03 — Research

Publication

A Sharp Numerical Approach for Modeling Flows in Reactive Porous Media

V. Poitier, E. Bayat, K. Hernandez Leyva, A. Sauret, F. Gibou

Journal of Computational Physics, 2026 · doi:10.1016/j.jcp.2026.115533

Navier–Stokes on adaptive quadtree grids, a level-set interface and a Stefan condition to follow precipitation and dissolution in a porous medium: how grains grow and merge, and how permeability and tortuosity change with the Péclet, Damköhler and Reynolds numbers. MSc thesis work at the University of California, Santa Barbara.
