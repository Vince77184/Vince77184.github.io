01 — Focus

What I work on

Commissioning & validation

Factory and site acceptance, verification on certified references, test plans for repeatability and drift, installation prerequisites, written procedures with clear acceptance criteria.

Test automation

Automated sequences in Python, LabVIEW and NI TestStand; servo-drives and instruments over Modbus TCP and CAN; NI data acquisition; safe fallback modes so a test never ends badly.

02 — Current work

Test & Commissioning Engineer

MSME laboratory (UMR 8208 CNRS), Université Gustave Eiffel · since September 2026

I am in charge of the laboratory's experimental platform: acoustic, permeability, thermal and multi-axial mechanical benches. I commission new instruments, automate the benches, run preventive and corrective maintenance (CMMS) and support researchers during their test campaigns.

Automation · Motion control

4-axis biaxial tensile rig

Four Festo servo-drives driven over Modbus TCP, NI force measurement and camera synchronisation for image correlation. I wrote the automated test sequences (Python, LabVIEW, NI TestStand) with a safe fallback mode, then a lab notebook and a delivery package so a new user can run the rig alone.

Next step on the same rig: motorising the shutter window, with a start-up dossier that separates what is established from what still has to be measured.

Commissioning · Acceptance

Hot Disk TPS 1500 thermal analyser

Commissioned at an industrial partner's site: manufacturer acceptance, verification on a certified reference material, then a test plan covering repeatability, drift and reference samples before the instrument went into service.

In parallel, installation prerequisites for about fifteen instruments (400 V three-phase, 8 bar air, cooling water, 2 t floor load, fire safety) and supplier comparison in France, Switzerland and the UK (€17k–100k), reported to the steering committee.

Procedures · Quality

Standardised test procedures

Four procedures written from the standards and from hands-on trials on the benches: impedance tube (ISO 10534-2), airflow resistivity (ISO 9053-1), open porosity, and transient plane source conductivity (ISO 22007-2). Each covers the method, acceptance criteria, quality checks and troubleshooting, so results stay comparable whoever runs the test.

Start-up review · Industrial communication

Microwave reactor bench

Technical review of a SAIREM MicroChem bench (0–200 W solid-state, 2.45 GHz) before start-up: hardware inventory, documentation, control software and its Modbus RTU / RS485 link, plus a beginner's guide for the team.

03 — Research

Publication

A Sharp Numerical Approach for Modeling Flows in Reactive Porous Media

V. Poitier, E. Bayat, K. Hernandez Leyva, A. Sauret, F. Gibou

Journal of Computational Physics, 2026 · doi:10.1016/j.jcp.2026.115533

Navier–Stokes on adaptive quadtree grids, a level-set interface and a Stefan condition to follow precipitation and dissolution in a porous medium: how grains grow and merge, and how permeability and tortuosity change with the Péclet, Damköhler and Reynolds numbers. MSc thesis work at the University of California, Santa Barbara.
